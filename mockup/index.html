MCCS PulseDesign review · Oct 29, 2026

Keep the dusk. Make the numbers speak sooner.

Pulse already has a voice no other dashboard has: the evening sky, the calm serif, one big number. This review keeps all of that and works on what gets in the way: the first number on Today undersells you because two unreported days count as zeros, controls stack three rows deep on a phone, labels fade out where the sky turns mauve, and a few screens (sheet, toast, Settings, report tiles) have rough edges.

Every screen below uses made-up sample numbers and posts. Nothing here is real MCCS data.

The changes

Ranked by what each one buys. 1 to 4 fix what staff and leadership misread or cannot read; 5 to 11 sharpen the craft; 12 to 14 close gaps on single screens.

    Not part of this mockup, but found during the review: when someone loads Pulse by importing Meta CSVs on a device that has never been unlocked, accounts that are hidden in the synced data (collaborator Pages) are not hidden, because the hide list only arrives with the passcode sync. Worth a small code fix whichever changes you pick.

    Methods: the live app was run locally at desktop (1440×900) and phone (390×844) sizes and every screen was inspected; findings were checked against the impeccable critique, better-interface (accessibility, layout, type, colour, polish), taste-skill’s app-screen rules and the frontend-design guidance, without stripping Pulse’s house style. Contrast figures are WCAG ratios computed from the app’s own colour tokens.
