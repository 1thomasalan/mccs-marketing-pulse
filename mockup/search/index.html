Try the new search

This page runs the real search engine on invented sample posts. Type in the box, or tap a query:

  1. Exact words beat partial words, which beat typos. Words next to each other in your order get a bonus.
  2. Every word must match somewhere: caption, hashtags, account, post type or date (“golf october”). Commas mean OR: “golf, swim”.
  3. Forgiving: any case, any order, “&” = “and”, accents and punctuation ignored, one typo in 4-7 letter words, two in longer ones. Typos only kick in when the word isn't in your data, so real words stay exact.
  4. Weak matches are trimmed so a typo never floods the list. Sorting switches to “Best match” while you search; you can still sort by views.
  5. New: the search icon at the top searches posts, web pages, email campaigns and signage at once.
